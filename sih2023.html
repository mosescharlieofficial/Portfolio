About Smart India Hackathon 2023

Smart India Hackathon is a nationwide initiative by the Ministry of Education and AICTE to provide students with a platform to solve some of the pressing problems we face in our daily lives, and thus inculcate a culture of product innovation and a mindset of problem-solving.

Hardware Edition Focus

The Hardware Edition of SIH 2023 focused on developing innovative hardware solutions to real-world problems. Participants worked on creating physical prototypes, embedded systems, and IoT devices to address challenges across various domains including agriculture, healthcare, smart cities, and environmental sustainability.

Our team "Innovative Idea" was selected among the top 5 teams nationally in the Hardware Edition Grand Finale, competing against thousands of teams from across India. We worked on Problem Statement SIH1476, developing a hardware-based solution that demonstrated technical excellence and practical applicability.

The 5-day Grand Finale was an intense experience that involved:

  • Prototype development and refinement
  • Technical implementation and testing
  • Presentation to expert judges
  • Collaboration with industry mentors
  • Networking with fellow innovators
