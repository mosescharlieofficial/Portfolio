About This Certification

The School Innovation Ambassador Training Program (SIATP) is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) in collaboration with AICTE and CBSE. This program aims to nurture a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students and teachers across India.

Design Thinking & Innovation Training

The 26-hour intensive training program focused on Design Thinking methodology, which is a human-centered approach to innovation that integrates the needs of people, the possibilities of technology, and the requirements for business success.

As part of the "Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav" celebrations, this program represents the government's commitment to fostering innovation and preparing the next generation of problem-solvers and innovators in India.

By completing this program, I have gained valuable skills in:

  • Understanding user needs and empathy mapping
  • Problem definition and ideation techniques
  • Prototyping and testing innovative solutions
  • Implementing design thinking in educational contexts
  • Fostering innovation culture in academic institutions
